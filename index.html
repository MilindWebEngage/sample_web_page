On-device storage

3 SQLite databases, 3 SharedPreferences files and the in-memory DataHolder. WebEngage.deleteAllData() (run on a license-code change) wipes everything except PERSISTENT_PREFS.

Category system. Custom events from track() are category application. HIGH · flush → BufferFlushStrategy (uploaded immediately); buffered → BufferNetworkStrategy; not uploaded → INTERNAL_EVENT (no ReportingController).
